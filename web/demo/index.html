A magnifying lens for any website

Draw a circle twice anywhere on this page and a lens opens at the pointer. Move to read through it, scroll under it (it zooms out a little while things rush past), and draw two more circles or press Esc to close it.

The fine print is where a lens earns its keep: 11 px text, a light grey, set in a long line that nobody reads at arm's length. Circle it twice and it reads like a heading.
