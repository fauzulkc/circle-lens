# Code of conduct

Circle Lens is a small, friendly project, and we want it to stay that way for everyone who uses it or works on it, whatever their background, identity or experience.

**Do:**
- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent.
- Give and take feedback about the work, not the person.
- Credit others' ideas and work.

**Don't:**
- Harass, insult, demean or exclude anyone.
- Make unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Make personal or political attacks.
- Publish someone's private information without their permission.
- Keep doing something after being asked to stop.

This applies in issues, pull requests, discussions, and anywhere you represent the project.

**Reporting.** If something is wrong, use GitHub's **Report content** on the comment, issue or pull request (the "…" menu). GitHub's moderators handle it, and the maintainer acts on it. To reach the maintainer privately about conduct, open a private report from the repository's **Security** tab and say it's about conduct. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may remove comments, commits or other contributions that break this code, and may block people who keep doing so.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
